## What Halves collects
Nothing. Halves has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no online
features. It does not collect, send or share any personal or device information.

## What Halves stores on your device
Your progress (stars, best times, coins, the daily streak) and your settings (sound, music,
vibration, motion, backgrounds) are saved in the game's private storage on your device. They
never leave it. Uninstalling the game, clearing its data, or Settings > Reset progress deletes
them.

## Children
Halves is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes
If a future version adds a feature that uses your information, this page will be updated before
that version is released, and the game will ask for any consent the law requires.
